# Content and Experiences Strategy

## Core marketing idea
Travel By Grace should increasingly market the **whole trip experience** rather than only the transport leg.

For selected trips, especially Cape Town weekends, the customer should be able to imagine:
- Private travel
- Accommodation ideas
- Entertainment or event options
- Restaurants / attractions
- Flexible itinerary planning

The business does not need to claim that every component is owned or directly sold by Travel By Grace. The value is coordination and personalised planning.

## Cape Town weekend theme
Strong concepts discussed include:
- Casino-linked entertainment weekends where venues host shows or events
- Theatre and musicals
- Plays and comedy
- Special events
- Food / nightlife where appropriate
- Relaxed luxury touches
- Scenic stops or attractions around the main entertainment reason for travel

## Garden Route content
The Garden Route should remain visible even while Cape Town weekends are being developed. Content can include:
- Airport transfers
- Family travel
- Visitor transport
- Local attractions
- Coastal experiences
- Private day trips

## Event publishing rules
- Use real upcoming event information.
- Prefer official venue/event sources.
- Show dates clearly.
- Avoid presenting third-party availability as guaranteed.
- Review dates and availability regularly.
- Remove or archive expired events.

## Planning horizon
A rolling several-month window is useful for promoting weekend-trip ideas. The replacement-site concept used September 2026 through January 2027 as an initial example window.

## Future research task
Maintain a recurring research backlog for:
- Cape Town theatre and musicals
- Casino-hosted shows / entertainment
- Garden Route theatre and events
- Seasonal festivals
- Attractive accommodation combinations
- Notable special events that justify a road trip

## Content principle
Do not make the site feel like an event-listing directory. Events are inspiration and conversion hooks for the Travel By Grace trip-planning service.
