# Brand and Vision

## Brand idea
Travel By Grace should feel like travelling with someone local who genuinely helps make the trip work. It is not positioned as a faceless booking engine or large bus-tour company.

## Positioning
Personalised private travel and trip planning from George and the Garden Route, with curated Cape Town travel and entertainment weekends available by special request.

## Brand promise
Comfortable, personal travel with help coordinating the trip around the customer's needs.

## Experience pillars
- Personal service
- Locally owned
- Comfortable private travel
- Flexible planning
- Coastal / Garden Route identity
- Fun road-trip energy
- A touch of luxury
- Memorable experiences rather than transport alone

## Tone
Warm, confident, practical and inviting. Avoid corporate tourism jargon, exaggerated luxury claims and language that makes the business sound larger than it is.

## Visual personality
The visual system should combine:
- Purple as the recognisable anchor colour
- Coastal freshness
- Sunset / entertainment energy
- Strong photography of Lucille, the vehicle and destinations
- Occasional bold accent colour for surprise and excitement

The site may use energetic motion or brighter colour, but trust and legibility remain more important than novelty.

## Portrait principle
Lucille is central to the trust story. Use an authentic portrait. Brightening and careful enhancement are welcome; changing facial identity or producing an uncanny artificial beauty treatment is not.

## Vehicle principle
The vehicle is part of the experience and should communicate comfort and practicality. Current website copy should not over-emphasise model year/model naming unless there is a specific customer reason to do so.

## Logo
No final logo is locked. Logo development may continue, but future designs should remain fun, travel-oriented and suitable for a locally owned private service.

## Audience priorities
1. Garden Route / George visitors and families needing private transport.
2. International visitors wanting a more personal local service.
3. Customers interested in Cape Town special-request trips.
4. Couples or small groups attracted by curated weekend entertainment experiences.

## Trust signals to develop
- Google Business profile
- Google reviews
- Authentic customer testimonials
- Clear contact details
- Real vehicle photography
- Real Lucille portrait
- Clear privacy and booking terms
- Simple, direct WhatsApp enquiry
