# Decisions Register

_Last consolidated: 2026-09-02_

## Current decisions

- Travel By Grace is positioned as a personalised, locally owned travel and trip-planning service.
- George and the Garden Route are the primary operating focus.
- Cape Town is available by special request and is strategically important for curated weekend trips.
- The website is primarily an enquiry/credibility site, not an e-commerce platform.
- WhatsApp and phone are primary contact methods; contact form/email are secondary.
- Pricing is mostly quote-on-request.
- Airport transfers are offered by prior arrangement; fixed prices may be added later.
- Deposit now / final payment later is acceptable as a booking pattern.
- Privacy policy may remain general for now.
- Booking terms should remain flexible and subject to confirmation with the owner.
- Google reviews should be added once the relevant business/review profile is available.
- Real supplied photos should be preferred over generic stock imagery.
- Lucille's portrait should be enhanced carefully without materially changing her appearance.
- Purple is the current core visual direction.
- High-impact accent colours may be added, but should support rather than overwhelm the purple system.
- The next website update must use `main` as the starting point and remain limited to portrait + colour-theme work unless separately approved.

## Decision hygiene
When a newer explicit user decision conflicts with an older note, the newer decision wins. The older idea should be moved to the archive rather than silently deleted, so the history remains understandable.
