# Operations and Booking Notes

## Enquiries
Primary customer contact should remain simple:
- WhatsApp
- Phone
- Contact form / email

## Service model
Travel By Grace is flexible and personalised rather than a rigid packaged-tour operator.

Services may include:
- Airport transfer by prior arrangement
- Private transport in George / Garden Route
- Cape Town travel by special request
- Trip planning
- Accommodation ideas / coordination
- Selected itinerary planning

## Pricing
- General approach: quote on request.
- Airport transfer prices may be published once finalised.
- Do not invent fixed prices before the owner approves them.

## Payment assumptions
- Deposit followed by final payment later is acceptable.
- Cash and instant-payment approaches have been discussed.
- Exact payment instructions should be confirmed per booking and should not be overstated on the public site until operationally final.

## Availability and scope
- Services are subject to prior arrangement and owner confirmation.
- Do not imply guaranteed availability.
- Do not promise destinations or services that have not been approved.

## Booking terms direction
Booking terms can remain practical and lightweight for now, making clear that:
- Trips are confirmed with the owner.
- Final arrangements depend on customer requirements.
- Third-party accommodation/events remain subject to their own availability and rules.

## Privacy direction
A general privacy statement is sufficient for the current simple enquiry site, provided it accurately describes contact-form/customer-data handling.
