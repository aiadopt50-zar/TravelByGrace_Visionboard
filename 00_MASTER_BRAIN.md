# Travel By Grace — Master Brain

_Last consolidated: 2026-09-02_

## What Travel By Grace is

Travel By Grace is a locally owned, personalised private travel and trip-planning service based around George and the Garden Route, with Cape Town travel available by special request and increasingly important for curated weekend experiences.

The brand should feel coastal, relaxed, personal and trustworthy, but not sleepy. The experience should carry a sense of excitement, colour and occasion — especially when selling entertainment weekends, shows, casino-linked entertainment, theatre, food, accommodation and road-trip experiences.

## Core customer promise

Make a trip easier by coordinating the moving parts around the customer rather than selling a rigid package. The customer should feel that Lucille is helping them put together a comfortable, enjoyable trip with private transport, accommodation ideas and selected itinerary items.

## Primary audiences

- Families
- International visitors
- Couples / small groups seeking a private trip
- Garden Route visitors needing airport or local transport
- Customers interested in Cape Town weekend experiences and entertainment trips

## Sales model

The site is primarily a credibility and enquiry tool, not an e-commerce store.

Primary conversion paths:
1. WhatsApp
2. Phone
3. Contact form / email

Pricing is generally quote-on-request. Airport transfer prices may be added to the site later when finalised.

Customers may pay a deposit and settle the remaining amount later. Cash and instant payment have been considered acceptable payment approaches. Exact booking arrangements remain subject to the owner and trip requirements.

## Visual direction

Purple is now the core colour direction.

The user also wants stronger, more surprising accent colours — "shocking" enough to create energy and memorability, but still usable in a travel brand. The likely design approach is a purple foundation with strategic electric accents rather than making every section neon.

Potential accent families to explore in the next website task:
- Electric magenta / hot pink
- Vivid coral
- Sunset orange
- Bright turquoise / cyan
- Acid-lime in very small doses
- Warm gold for premium cues

Any final colour changes must be tested against readability, accessibility and Lucille's photos.

Lucille should remain recognisably herself. Enhancements should be restrained: brighten, improve perceived clarity/contrast and presentation, and avoid altering her features or making the portrait look artificially generated.

## Content strategy

A key growth concept is selling the whole **trip idea**, not isolated transport.

For Cape Town, the stronger marketing concept is:

> Private travel + accommodation ideas + selected itinerary / entertainment items as one coordinated weekend experience.

Events and experiences should be based on real upcoming shows, theatre, casino entertainment, attractions and seasonal opportunities. Because event details are controlled by third parties, dates and availability need regular review.

## Brand permanence vs website experiments

The brand, company vision, customer promise and operating principles belong in this Brain repo and should survive every website redesign.

Specific layouts, animations, hero concepts and UI treatments belong to individual website versions/branches and should not be treated as permanent brand decisions.
